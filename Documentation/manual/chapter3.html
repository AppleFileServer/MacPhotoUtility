The Window

Everything happens in one window. On the left, the queue of photographs and the details of the one you’re working on; in the middle, the photograph with its frame, and the two previews; on the right, the output settings and the export buttons.

The window’s title is the project’s name, or Untitled. Closing the window quits MacPhoto Utility, asking first if there are unsaved changes.

The Queue

The list of photographs you’re working on, and the details of the one selected.

PartWhat it does
+ buttonAdds photographs (as Add Photographs).
Folder buttonAdds the photographs in a folder (as Add Folder).
A rowA small picture, the name, the print’s orientation (P or L) and its state: not looked at yet, looked at, small for its output, can’t be read, exported, missing.
The countHow many photographs, how many can’t be read, and how many files were skipped as not photographs (click it to see which).
RemoveTakes the selected photographs out of the queue (Delete does the same).
Remove AllEmpties the queue.
The detailsThe selected photograph’s kind, its size as stored and upright, how the camera turned it, any turns you’ve made, and when it was last exported. For a missing photograph, where it was, with Locate and Locate in Folder.

The Picture and Its Frame

The photograph, turned the right way up, with the frame you’re setting drawn on it and everything outside the frame dimmed.

ControlWhat it does
Print Crop, Screen CropWhich frame you’re setting (Command-1, Command-2, or Tab). The other shows as a dashed line.
Turn buttonsTurn the photograph a quarter to the left or right (Command-[, Command-]).
Portrait, LandscapeWhich way the print stands. Shown only with Print Crop chosen.
Zoom sliderMakes the frame smaller (to the right) or larger, keeping its shape: from the largest the photograph allows to an eighth of that. A pinch or the scroll wheel does the same.
ResetThe frame back to the middle at full size (Command-0).
The frameDrag inside it to move it, or a corner to size it. The arrow keys move it a little at a time.

The Previews

Both are made from the whole photograph, a moment after each change: they’re the pictures themselves, not sketches.

PartWhat it shows or does
PrintThe print image, scaled to fit: its size in pixels, its resolution, and any warning.
Mac Plus screenThe screen image, one dot of the Plus’s screen to each point, in black and white.
LargerThe screen image twice the size.
Brightness, Contrast−100 to +100 each, for the screen image only. Each photograph keeps its own.
NormalBrightness and contrast back to 0.

Output and Export

What to make, where, and the buttons that make it.

ControlWhat it does
360 PPI, 300 PPIThe print’s resolution. The framing is the same either way.
Print PICTThe print image: 8-bit grey, for MacPhoto.
Screen PICTThe screen image: 1-bit, 512 by 342.
MacPaintThe screen image on a MacPaint page, in the middle.
Folder, ChooseWhere the files go, in folders inside it named for the Plus.
Export AllEvery photograph that can be read, one at a time (Shift-Command-E), with progress and Stop.
Export This PhotographThe selected one (Command-E).

These settings are shared by every photograph, kept in a project, and remembered between times you use MacPhoto Utility.

The File and Photograph menus hold MacPhoto Utility’s own commands.

File menuKeys

Open RecentThe projects opened lately.
Add Photographs…⌘IAdds photographs to the queue.
Add Folder…⇧⌘IAdds the photographs in a folder.
Save, Save As…⌘S, ⇧⌘SSaves the project.
Export This Photograph⌘EExports the selected photograph.
Export All⇧⌘EExports every photograph.
Photograph menuKeys
Turn Left, Turn Right⌘[, ⌘]Turns the photograph a quarter.
Edit Print Crop, Edit Screen Crop⌘1, ⌘2Chooses which frame you’re setting.
Reset Crop⌘0That frame back to the middle at full size.

The MacPhoto Utility, Edit, Window and Help menus are the ones every Mac program has: About MacPhoto Utility and Quit are in the first.

The Files It Makes

In the folder you choose, MacPhoto Utility makes a folder for each kind of file, named as it will be on the Plus.

FolderFileWhat it is
Print Filesname.pictThe print image: a PICT in 256 greys, at the print’s exact size. About 1 MB.
PICT Filesname.pictThe screen image: a black-and-white PICT, 512 by 342. Under 23 KB.
MacPaint Filesname.MACThe screen image in the middle of a MacPaint page. Under 52 KB.
manifest.jsonA record of every file exported into the folder: from which photograph, framed how, and its checksum. For your records; the Plus doesn’t need it.

Names are made to suit the Plus: up to 31 characters with the ending, without colons, and with letters the Plus can’t show changed for the nearest it can. A project, saved where you choose, ends in .macphoto.
