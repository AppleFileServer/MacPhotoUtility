# Figures

Made by `tools/manual/make-figures.py` into `figures/png` (MG7). Codes:
**w-** a whole window, **p-** a part of it (cut square, grey rule),
**a-** an alert or dialog, **f-** other macOS screens (Finder, panels),
**m-** a menu, **plus-** a Plus screen (Mini vMac at 1×), **d-** a
drawing, **c<n>-opener** an opener spread (two halves).

The captures are private (`~/MacPlusProjects/manual-assets-private/
macphoto-screenshots/`), taken from B-0.9.0 with Jack's ten sample
photographs (MQ1); the openers' photographs too (`macphoto/openers/`).
